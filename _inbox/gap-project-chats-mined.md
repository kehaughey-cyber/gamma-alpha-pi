## 1. Transmission theory + the `transmission-forge` skill
*(chat: "Failure as a tool for narrowing possibility")*

The transmission-theory framework was built into a **working, installable skill** here. This
is significant IP, and the actual files are **cloud-only** (downloadable from this chat:
`transmission-forge` .skill, `SKILL.md`, `verifiers.md` — Save/Download buttons present).

**The three laws** (how a story flies and stays loaded across generations):
- **Propulsion** — double emotional payment, to teller and receiver, on the spot.
- **The weld** — cargo fused into the events, so a selfish teller cannot strip it without
  breaking the story he needs intact.
- **Seating** — cargo lodged in universal human material, not local context that sheds in
  transit. **Domain-truth** is part of seating: if the story's mechanism is factually wrong,
  it throws out anyone who knows that domain, exactly at the load-bearing beat.

**The skill** (`transmission-forge`, SKILL.md ~85 lines): a five-stage process —
**extract payload → seat → weld → tune propulsion → self-gate** — with an "ordering leash"
that stops gossip-drift, emits the **skeleton as a first-class artifact** so copies are
trackable, and encodes field-test logic (copy-fidelity gated by shares; skeleton-matching to
find renamed thieves).

**`verifiers.md`** makes two verifiers explicit and repeatable (so they survive context loss):
- **The selfish-teller test** (five deformations) — deform the story to strip its cargo; the
  weld lives at whatever beat kills the cargo when removed. The primary quality gate.
- **The domain-truth test** — does the mechanism survive contact with someone who knows the
  trade? Added after the worked example got a lock mechanism factually wrong.

**Load-bearing honest seams (Kevin's, kept in the skill's own text):**
- **AI is an ~80% solution** — fluent in a domain's vocabulary, unreliable on how the parts
  assemble, and states the wrong assembly with the same confidence as the right one. The
  skill does not pretend to be a push-button generator; it builds a scaffold and forces the
  human check at the mechanism seam.
- **Step 1 (payload extraction) is the upstream failure point** — a well-built vessel around a
  hollow payload passes all three structural laws, so a summary dressed as a payload welds to
  air and no verifier catches it. Harden this next.
- **The measurement half is unbuilt** — the skill makes trackable skeletons, but finding
  copies in the wild / scoring fidelity at scale is the build-last infrastructure, still last.

**Worked example:** "The Locksmith's Apprentice" — the old locksmith who *smiles* at each dead
pin (each a row he'll never touch again); the boy counts them as losses, the master as
elimination. Payload: reward tracks how much possibility you destroyed, not whether you won.
(Note: the lock mechanism as told is not how pin-tumblers work — this is the example that
produced the domain-truth verifier.)

→ **Migrate:** pull the three files to a `skills/` or `tools/` lane. The three laws belong
near the corpus/transmission doctrine (§0 of `WBS.md`).

## 2. Product / GTM — Etsy competitive comps + productization strategy
*(chat: "Parallel product examples and offerings")*

Three Etsy listings logged as **competitive comps** (the commodity end of the men's-dev market):
- **Men's Purpose & Discipline Workbook** (self-guided PDF)
- **The Modern Man's Mission Board** (100 "real" prompts/goals)
- **90-Day Life Reset Workbook** (personal development)

**What they share, and why they are what GAP is *not*:** instant-download PDF, low price, no
gate, no trust network, no intergenerational structure, generic framing that announces itself
fully at the point of sale. You buy the container and get the whole thing at the door. The
opposite of GAP's core: the framework does not announce itself; a man arrives at understanding
through process; the thing is a metaskill carried inside a brotherhood, not a container handed
over at checkout.

**The strategic move these are comps for:** only the **3-Day Reset** and **Field Manual
excerpts** could live in this instant-download format without violating the sequenced-
revelation architecture — they are already "how," already prescriptive, meant to be worked
rather than arrived at. That is a plausible **top-of-funnel**: a cheap, real, useful artifact
that does its job and points toward the larger thing without revealing it.

**The failure mode to avoid:** productizing the wrong layer (Philosophy, NTI, the Coin) into a
$9 PDF flattens a sequenced revelation and kills the receivability the whole design depends on.

→ Feeds `companion/reference/layer4-intelligence.md` (product strategy) and the MVP entry: the
3-Day Reset / Threshold Reading as the honest top-of-funnel.
