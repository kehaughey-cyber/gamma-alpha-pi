# The User Journey (the walkable loop)

How a man moves through Gamma Alpha Pi, why it is built this way, and what each
part is for. This is the design document the corpus, the companion, and the app
all serve.

This is the *design logic*, the why. Its concrete instantiation, the phase-by-phase
choreography of what a man actually walks through (the Interview, the Mine, the Walk,
the Forge), is `forge-journey.md`. Read them together.

## The four things that stop every man

Regardless of the arena of his life, a man is stopped by four things. Desire for
change is nearly universal; these are what stand between the desire and the change.

1. **Fear** — of the unknown, and of change itself.
2. **Not starting.**
3. **Not finishing.**
4. **Missing information** — he does not know how.

The whole system is the structural answer to these four.

## What answers each

- **Fear** is answered by the **big brother**: recognize the fear, be the mirror
  that reflects it back without shame, and be living proof a man crossed the same
  unknown and is standing on the other side. Draw him forward with a future of
  agency and strength. You do not push a man through fear; you pull him with who he
  becomes. The mirror is also where self-honesty enters: self-deception is the
  disguise fear wears (the reasonable case laid over the real fear), and the mirror
  strips it.
- **Not starting** is ignition, a one-time threshold crossed once: the commitment
  ritual (he takes the coin), and one first action that cannot be walked back.
- **Not finishing** is retention, a thousand small thresholds: the big brother
  noticing the stall, accountability, and visible progress (the coin lighting a
  segment). Starting and finishing share components but are different jobs; a guide
  runs them differently.
- **Missing information** is answered by the **six books**, and critically by
  sequence and just-in-time delivery. Information has two failure modes: too little
  (he doesn't know how) and too much at once (he drowns in the library). The
  ordered corpus and the companion handing the right tool at the right moment
  answer the second.

## The corpus sequence (and why the order is load-bearing)

1. **Coin** — orient in the world (symbolism, history, philosophy, theology, math,
   cosmology). A way of seeing. The ground the inward turn requires.
2. **Brother** — know thyself. Formation. What you want, believe, and are, examined
   rather than inherited.
3. **Field Manual** — the tools, converted to motion (mission, goals, planning,
   habits, rhetoric, leadership, tested in work, home, money, body).
4. **Nosce Te Ipsum** — the workbook. Build the specific, honest architecture of
   your actual life. Revisited at every life transition.
5. **Father** — fatherhood and the long view (sibling in voice to the Big Brother).
6. **Big Brother** — the guide manual: run the walk for another man.

Worst case, if everything else fell away, the books could stand alone with the
Bible and a man with time. By design they are meant to operate inside a fraternity,
with a big brother, and an app.

## The self-enforcing gate (the core mechanism)

The stages are not checkpoints a guide adjudicates. They are **self-enforcing**.
If a man answers honestly, he knows the next step and takes it. If he lies to
himself, he stalls, and no one, not the guide, not the author, can make him drink.
The corpus is architected so the same questions recur, each pass breaching a little
more territory, wearing down the evasions until honesty is the path of least
resistance by the last touch. The intelligence is in the architecture (the
recursion), not in the person running it. That is what lets the system replicate
without depending on one wise guide.

Consequence: the gate enforces **honesty**, not **persistence**. A man who won't
get honest stalls, correctly. But a willing man can also quietly drift off before
the recursion finishes. Nothing in the text reaches out and holds him. That gap is
the big brother's job.

## Two tracks, one program

- **Learner track:** a man moves through the corpus and changes.
- **Guide track:** a changed man learns to run the learner track for someone else.

The guide track is not a separate program built later. It is the back half of the
learner track, formalized. A man becomes a guide not by finishing the corpus but by
demonstrating he can be the presence, mirror, and proof for a newer man. The system
produces its own next builders as a byproduct of men finishing. One replicating
level (learner → guide) is enough to go from three men to hundreds; guide-certifies-
guide is a later concern, not an MVP one.

## The big brother's real job

Not assessor of the crossing (self-enforced). Witness, presence, accountability,
and the relationship that makes a man want to keep showing up long enough for the
honesty to arrive. The gate handles "is he ready." The brother handles "is he still
here." What that job looks like per problem is the content of the Book of the Big
Brother (`corpus/big-brother/OUTLINE.md`).

## Sequencing (what to build when)

The corpus already carries the self-enforcing gates through its recursion; that is
mostly transcription to make explicit, not new authoring. The Big Brother book is
lighter than a full guide-certification apparatus because there is no crossing to
judge. The app is deferred by design, not cut: its record and accountability
functions come first (accountability answers the real failure mode, drift), and its
recursive-improvement meta-data is earned only once men are actually walking. The
leanest form of the app is this repo plus a companion driven by `companion/`.
