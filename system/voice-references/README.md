# Voice references — the concrete standard

`../voice.md` states the house voice in the abstract. These are the fingerprints: true
stories from Kevin's life that show how the voice actually moves, from the concrete to the
transcendent, owning weight without performing it, earning the point before making it.
They were written to be loaded into an AI project before it writes or rewrites a word, to
calibrate its ear. Read them the same way.

- `the-extension-cord.md` — faith as a decision in the dark (the 2007–2008 collapse).
- `the-hill-with-the-ashes.md` — loss, fire, and the beginning of a walk with God.

The Book of the Father remains the voice standard in the corpus itself (`../voice.md`,
`corpus/father/`); these are the lived exemplars behind it. Source:
`Fraternity/GAP/Voice_Reference_*.docx`.
