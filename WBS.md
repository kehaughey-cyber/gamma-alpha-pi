## The one honest read

The original WBS is a generic org-buildout and **undercounts GAP's real work**, which is the
**content and the transmission method** (§0) — that is where the differentiator and most of
the unmigrated production live. The MVP correctly ignores most of the generic WBS (Steps
5-scale) and nests into only four of its sections (3 Branding, 4 Recruitment, 9 Evaluation,
plus the companion tool). The nearest thing to a blocking gap that the MVP does *not* fully
cover is the **entry** (the Threshold Reading) and **replication** (the Big Brother book) —
both content, both in §0, both surfaced from the cloud project.
