# Copy Audit — the front door vs the canon

Does the public site say the true thing and route a man to the walk? This checks the
`site-plan.md` rebuild against (a) the current live site and (b) the now-curated repo canon.
Short version: the plan is strong and largely aligned; one real tension to settle, and a
live-site verification that needs your login.

## The plan vs the current live site (what the rebuild changes)

The live site today (per `channels.md` and a read of the homepage) runs Home / About /
Join Us / Books-Coin-Merch / Contact / Program List / More, with a PayPal donate button.
The plan changes that to a tighter funnel:

- **Nav → five pages**, one path: Home / About / The Journey / Join Us / Books, Coin and Merch. Contact moves to the footer; Program List is renamed **The Journey** and resequenced.
- **Adds the sorting mechanism** on Join Us: the written interview creed, one required long-text question ("what is happening in your life right now that made you stop and find this page?"), a renamed submit ("Take the First Step"), and a personal autoresponse ("You knocked."). The plan flags this as the single most important change.
- **Removes the PayPal donate button** from all pages.
- **Dark/gold visual rebuild**, mobile-first, one clear next step per page ("Knock").

These are real changes, not cosmetic — the current site sells and lists; the plan sorts and routes. That is the Step-4 gap closing.

## The one tension to settle — the life arc

The About page uses **both** arcs, on the same page:
- Section 1 subhead: "Rooted in the Greek words for **Son, Brother, Father**" (the 3-stage arc — this is literally what ΓΑΠ means).
- Section 3 "Every Man's Journey": "**Son. Brother. Husband. Father. King. Elder.**" (the 6-stage seasons).

Per the reconciliation in `system/forge-journey.md`, the 3-stage is canonical (it is the coin symbol, Constitution §2.4, the corpus) and the 6 are finer seasons layered on it. As written, the page reads as two different schemes. **Recommendation:** keep the name explanation as Son/Brother/Father (fixed), and if you want the six seasons in "Every Man's Journey," add one line making the relationship explicit (the three stages, lived across these seasons) so it does not read as a contradiction. Your call whether to keep the six publicly at all.
