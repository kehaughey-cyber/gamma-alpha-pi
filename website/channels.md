## Open items for the Step-4 launch audit

- **Consistency of message.** Do all five surfaces say the same true thing the current
  corpus and system say, in the house voice? A funnel that flatters betrays the thing.
- **The handoff seam.** Where does each surface route a man next, and does that path lead to
  the walk (intake → the companion → a big brother), or does it dead-end at a purchase?
- **Handle hygiene.** The YouTube handle reads `@GammaAlphPI` (note the spelling); confirm
  it is intended and consistent with the others before printing it anywhere.
- **Discord as operations.** The Discord is also an `operations/` concern (it is a real
  container for walking brothers). Document its structure there when the operations ledger
  is stood up.
