# Archive

The raw source material for Gamma Alpha Pi lives in a separate folder, deliberately
kept out of this repo:

    C:\Users\Dad\OneDrive\Desktop\Active\Fraternity

It holds years of drafts, old book revisions, research, marketing, meeting notes,
the GAP companion specs, media (presentation videos, ambience footage), and the
constitution/bylaws. It is the quarry. This repo is the cut stone.

Do not bulk-import the archive. Pull specific material in on purpose, convert it to
Markdown, and file it in the right place (or stage it through `_inbox/` first).
Notable seams worth mining next:

- `Fraternity/GAP/` — companion architecture, system prompt, the Forge user-journey
  drafts, voice references, layer pipelines. Curate into `companion/`.
- `Fraternity/Books/` — the `.docx` authoring originals for the corpus. Regenerate
  the `.md` here with `tools/docx_to_md.py` after each rewrite.
- `Fraternity/ideation/`, `Fraternity/Research/`, `Fraternity/Marketing/` — mine as
  needed; most is raw and should pass through `_inbox/`.
