# instruments — the tools a man fills in

The worksheets, assessments, and journals a man actually works through on the walk.
`operations/` runs the brotherhood; these are the objects a man puts his own answers
into. They are the physical form of the corpus's gates — the place where an honest
answer moves him and a dishonest one stalls him.

## What is here now
- **`self-assessment.md`** — the flagship: the NTI 45-aspect self-assessment (all 45 named,
  the current/desired/delta method, prioritization). The instrument the Mine runs on and the
  companion facilitates. (`Where I want to be.xlsx` was the same instrument, older/broken;
  superseded.)
- **`allostatic-audit.md`** — the six-category load audit; locates a man's position (Danger
  Zone / The Becoming / Atrophy).
- **`community-map.md`** — Getting to Know Your Community (the Community-aspect worksheet).

## Non-negotiables
- **These are blank instruments, never filled-in ones.** A man's answers are private to
  him by design (`companion/README.md`, job #2). The repo is public — only the empty
  template goes in; anyone's completed copy stays in the quarry, always.
- **An instrument is a gate, not a judge** (`system/user-journey.md`). It asks the
  question that moves a willing man and lets a dishonest answer stall him on its own.
  Do not add scoring that adjudicates whether his answer is "good enough." Watch for
  drift, never for the state of his soul.
- **Just-in-time.** Each instrument is handed to a man at the point in the walk it
  serves — note *where in the journey* it belongs when you file it.

## Build order
File the two that gate the earliest stages first (self-assessment, "where I want to be"),
since those are what a man meets right after intake.
