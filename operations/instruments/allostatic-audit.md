# The Allostatic Audit

A load worksheet that turns "stress" into a data set. A man scores his load across six
categories, sums it, weighs it against his recovery, and locates where he actually stands.
It converts an emotional reaction into a technical assessment a man can act on. Be radically
honest; you cannot build a high-performance life on neglected basics.

- **Source:** `Fraternity/GAP/The Allostatic Audit Worksheet.docx` (also `.pdf`).
- **Where it fits:** early, alongside the Mine. It surfaces the load a man is carrying
  before he designs what he is building. Blank template only; a filled copy is private to him.

## 1. Financial Load
Not net worth. Financial *peace of mind*. **Score (1–10): ___**
- Low (1–3): 6+ months liquid cash; lifestyle well below means; no pressure to signal wealth.
- Moderate (4–7): managed debt; small emergency fund; periodic pressure to keep up with peers.
- High (8–10): paycheck to paycheck; high-interest debt; constant pressure to maintain an image; avoiding bank accounts.

## 2. Relational Load
The physiological impact of your social circle. **Score (1–10): ___**
Dominant archetype(s):
- [ ] **The Friction Point** — active, ongoing conflict.
- [ ] **The Cold Storage** — silent "open tabs" from past hurt.
- [ ] **The Mirror Weight** — pressure to reflect success / fear of being seen as "less than."

Clinical checkpoint:
- Is your social circle a **Shared Beam** (support) or **Extra Cargo** (demand)?
- Does a specific name on your phone cause a physical tightening in your chest/gut? (Yes / No)

## 3. The "Later" List (Neglect Load)
The weight of what you are ignoring is often heavier than the weight of what you are doing.
**Score (1–10): ___**
- Low (1–2): high completion rate; basic home/body maintenance up to date.
- High (8–9): environment and body in managed decay (ignored repairs, missed appointments).

Triage action (choose one):
- **2-Minute Rule** — a <2 min task you do immediately after this audit: ___
- **Decision Date** — a major task you put on the calendar for a firm date: ___
- **Delegation** — a task you hire out or ask help with: ___

## 4. Professional Load
The physiological erosion of your career. **Score (1–10): ___**
- Frequency of urgency: how often do non-emergencies feel like life or death?
- Boundary erosion: checking work at meals, late night, on waking.
- Cognitive residue: long lead times to "stop the spin" after hours.
- Stimulant reliance: increasing caffeine/nicotine to maintain focus.

## 5. Cognitive Backlog
Your relationship with inputs and information velocity. **Score (1–10): ___**
- Input velocity: high-frequency screen checking (dopamine seeking).
- The "unfinished" count: open browser tabs and unread messages.
- Silence threshold: can you sit 5 minutes in silence without seeking distraction? (Yes / No)

## 6. Sleep Integrity
Restoration is the only way to upgrade your operating system. **Status: [ Pass / Fail ]**
Evaluate yourself 90 minutes after waking. If you require caffeine to function or feel an
early-afternoon crash, integrity is **Failed**.

## Final analysis
- **Total Load Score** (sum of categories 1–5): ___ / 50
- **Recovery Multiplier** (number of "Yes" answers in sections 2 & 6): ___ / 6

Locate your position:
- **The Danger Zone** (Load 35+ / Recovery 0–2): the Belly of the Whale. Mission: survival and triage. Stop winning; start breathing.
- **The Becoming** (Load 35+ / Recovery 4–6): the Road of Trials. Mission: consistency. You are being forged.
- **Atrophy** (Load <20 / Recovery 0–2): the Ordinary World. Mission: mobilization. Seek a challenge; build capacity.

**Narrative anchor:** "I am currently in ___ [stage]. This stress is not a character flaw; it is ___ [narrative explanation]."

**Tribal outreach:** Who is the first person you will contact to perform a "vulnerability exchange" and share these scores?
