# operations — running the brotherhood

The light machinery that keeps men moving. Simple by design; a spreadsheet beats an
app until the app is earned.

## Intake
Stage 0: capture who a man is when he arrives, and the fork-in-the-road commitment.
This is start-friction control; it is where "not starting" is first answered.

## Pairing and cadence
How a learner is matched to a big brother, the container (group chat, weekly call),
and the rhythm. Weekly is the default cadence for the MVP. You assign pairings.

## Ledger
Who is at what stage, who guides whom, and who has produced whom (the replication
signal: a little brother who became a big brother). A spreadsheet is the MVP. This
is how you see whether the loop is actually closing.

The ledger is stood up: **`ledger.md`** (the method, columns, stage vocabulary, cadence,
and the one number that matters) and **`ledger-template.csv`** (importable starting point).

**The live ledger stays private, off this public repo** — real brothers' names and contact
history are private formation data. Keep it in a private sheet; the repo holds only the
template and the method. See the privacy note at the top of `ledger.md`.

The one number that matters right now: does a little brother become a big brother.
Three brothers are walking. The loop is proven when one of them raises the next.
