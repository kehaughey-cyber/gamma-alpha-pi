## What it is (in one paragraph)

A "Leadership Intelligence System" that turns the signal every member journey generates into
institutional wisdom, so the institution that has served 1,000 men serves the 1,001st better
than anyone starting from zero could. Three components: an intelligence architecture (Part
II), a financial-stewardship framework (Part III), and a tool-creation pipeline (Part IV).

## Part II — Leadership Intelligence

Three tiers, meant to run at once: **descriptive** (what happened), **predictive** (who is
about to disengage or is ready to step up, flagged so a *human* makes contact — the
intervention is always human), and **prescriptive** (what the curriculum and leadership
should change). Outputs: a real-time dashboard, periodic reports (AI drafts, humans judge),
predictive flags ("a prompt to act, not a profile to judge"), curriculum-effectiveness
scoring, and a content-performance loop. Access is tiered by role.

## Part III — Financial Stewardship (generational viability)

GAP is not a profit center, and an institution that cannot sustain itself cannot sustain its
mission; the framework walks that line. Guards against three failure modes: financial
fragility, mission drift via money, and capture by private interest. Core diagnostics: CAC
by channel, member LTV in **two tracks read together** (financial LTV and *mission* LTV —
transformation, mentorship multiplier, generational re-entry), and four integrated readings
of finance-plus-mission. Backed by structural protections: a non-acquisition covenant and a
stewardship covenant every council member ratifies.

## Part IV — Tool-Creation Pipeline

How member experience becomes institutional capability. Signal from three sources (the
Companion in aggregate, structured brother/Big-Brother feedback, council review) enters one
governed pipeline of seven stages: signal detection → pattern validation → mission-alignment
review (the governing test) → design/development (GAP frameworks, built by men who lived it,
cohort-tested) → distribution decision → deployment → effectiveness measurement. Tools are
institutional assets; creators are credited, not owed revenue or veto.

## Why it is parked, precisely

Every capability here presumes infrastructure Phase 1 explicitly does not have: persistent
member data, a database, dashboards, a member base large enough for "statistically
meaningful" patterns. Building it before brothers are walking would be the project's
historical failure mode — elaborate systems before a working loop. When Phase 2 earns
persistent memory and the visual coin (see `../deployment.md`), the earliest, smallest slice
of this — a human-read flag when a walking brother goes quiet — is the first piece worth
standing up, because it serves the one metric that matters: does a little brother become a
big brother.
