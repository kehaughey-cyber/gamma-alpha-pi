# Reference: The Living Coin

The full 45-segment map and coin symbolism the Companion draws on. This is human-readable
reference behind the condensed versions embedded in `../system-prompt.md`. It is also the
map a brother is pointed to so he can name the segment he wants to work on. Canonical
symbolic depth lives in the Book of the Coin (`corpus/coin/book-of-the-coin.md`); this is
the operational digest.

- **Sources:** `Fraternity/GAP/GAP_Companion_Architecture.docx` (v2.0, the segment map),
  `..._Addendum_Coin_Symbolism.docx`, `..._Addendum_IAM_Three_Layers.docx` (all v1.0)

## Geometry (for the record)

The coin has **fifty segments of 7.2 degrees** each (50 × 7.2 = 360°), a nod to
Eratosthenes, who measured the Earth's circumference from a stick and a shadow by
reasoning, not travel. **Forty-five are fully visible; five are partially obscured** by the
triangle and compass points, the built-in humility of the coin: no man sees everything, and
the circle is still complete. The 45 visible segments carry the assessment.

> The older architecture doc describes 45 segments at 8° instead. Both coin addenda and the
> deployable system prompt use the 50/7.2/45-visible model; treat 45-at-8° as superseded.
> Kevin owns the physical coin and has the final word.

## The 45 visible segments

Three tenets, five constituents each, three sub-components each. A brother assesses each
sub-component with the three NTI questions (current state / desired state / life at goal).
Progression within a constituent is foundational-first (sub-components 1 and 2 before 3);
across constituents and tenets a man moves freely, the way real men grow.

**LOVE** — how a man gives and receives at every level of his life.
- **God** — the vertical relationship, the source from which horizontal love flows.
- **Self** — how a man stewards the life he was given; you cannot love others well otherwise.
- **Family** — presence, provision, protection, investment in their flourishing.
- **Community** — love extended outward past the household.
- **Creation** — stewardship of the world entrusted to his care.

**STRENGTH** — a man's developed capability across every domain.
- **Physical** — the first domain of discipline; intentionality, not athleticism.
- **Mental** — clarity, discernment, strategic capacity.
- **Emotional** — mastery of feeling, not suppression; relational capacity.
- **Spiritual** — purpose, transcendence, the anchoring vertical relationship.
- **Financial** — provision, stewardship, generational legacy.

**VISION** — a man's clarity about where he is going and why.
- **Personal** — his own life, goals, and the alignment of daily action to aspiration.
- **Faith** — how faith guides his direction.
- **Family** — where his family is going and what he is doing to get them there.
- **Community** — his responsibility beyond his own household.
- **Creative** — the creative contribution God placed in him.

The three sub-components under each subject are named in full in the fillable instrument,
`operations/instruments/self-assessment.md` (all 45, e.g. Love→God→{Seek God First, Obey His
Commands, Constant Communication}). That file is the assessment; this is the map.

## The coin's four meanings

A **touchstone** (physical weight in the pocket that says this is real), a **gift** (given
to someone he loves, it hands over his standard, a covenant in physical form), a **map**
(the compass rose and north star he builds inside himself through the NTI work), and a
**progress record and reminder** (lit and unlit segments that orient rather than judge, over
the words I AM WITH BROTHERS). The governing rule for the Companion: never explain a symbol
and leave it floating; always land it in the brother's life.

## Symbolic vocabulary (digest)

- **Triangle and hourglass** — the three tenets (Love at the base, Strength and Vision at
  the sides) around the hourglass of time; what a man builds in the three is what remains.
- **Compass rose and north star** — external orientation on the coin, internal orientation
  built through Nosce Te Ipsum (values/principles, and Vision as the fixed point).
- **The languages of the world** — man in every tongue; the call is universal, the path worn.
- **Ezekiel 22:30 / Isaiah 6:8** — "I looked for someone to stand in the gap" / "Here am I,
  send me": the complete arc of the calling, the coin the record of preparation.
- **I AM** (top of the coin) — three layers at once: the divine name (Exodus 3:14), the
  subconscious instruction (what follows I AM becomes identity), and the declaration (I AM
  WITH BROTHERS). Used in session as an anchor and, in crisis, spoken as truth. Full
  treatment is embedded in `../system-prompt.md`.

## The doctrine line this map must hold

The segments are **not a judge**. The brother assesses himself; the Companion receives,
reflects, deepens, and watches whether the gap is closing, over time. In Phase 1 nothing is
"lit" or tracked. The credential idea from the architecture doc (segments lighting to make a
man a findable resource, gating who may mentor) is **deferred and unresolved**: it edges
toward adjudicating a man's state, which the system is built never to do. Keep it as an open
design question, not a Phase-1 behavior. See `system/user-journey.md`.
