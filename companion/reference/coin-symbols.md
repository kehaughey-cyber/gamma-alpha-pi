# Reference: The Coin's Symbols (quick index)

A one-line-each index to the coin's symbols, front and back — the fast lookup for "what
does that symbol mean." **It is not the canon.** The full symbology of the coin lives in
**the Book of the Coin (`corpus/coin/book-of-the-coin.md`)**, which carries a dedicated
chapter for nearly every element listed here. When the meaning matters, read the book;
this index only points the way. The companion's *conversational* use of these is in
`system-prompt.md`; the visual faces are in `brand/` (`coin-front.png`, `coin-back.png`).

- **Authority:** `corpus/coin/book-of-the-coin.md` (the full treatment, ~70k words).
- **This index drawn from:** the book's chapter list plus `Fraternity/Coin/Symbol
  explinations.xlsx` (research; superseded by the book where they differ).

## Concepts (the words on the coin)

- **God** — the divine creator and supreme being; the source at the top of the hierarchy of purpose.
- **Self** — individuality and identity; the self a man must know before he can lead.
- **Family** — the foundation of support and love; the first community he is responsible for.
- **Community** — shared identity and belonging; his sphere of influence beyond the family.
- **I AM** — self-existence and identity; the divine name (Exodus 3:14) and the declaration a man makes of himself. (Three layers: `system-prompt.md`.)
- **With Brothers** — unity, camaraderie, mutual support; the last line of the Creed, a fact and a declaration.
- **Nosce Te Ipsum** — "Know Thyself"; self-awareness and introspection, the inward turn.
- **1024** — the direct-line ancestors ten generations back; the web of lineage a man carries (cf. the Walk, `system/forge-journey.md`).

## The languages of man (the civilizations ring)

The word for *man*, one per civilization, ringing the back: the call is universal, the path
is worn. Babylonia (𒇽), Egyptian, Mongol (ᠡᠷᠡᠭᠲᠡᠢ), Spanish (Hombre), Russian (мужчина),
Umayyad & Abbasid (رجل), Hindu-Buddhist (वीर), Ottoman (Adam), Persian (مرد), Maya, China,
British (Man), Roman (Vir), Greek (Ανδρας). The empires' histories behind them stay in the
quarry (research behind the Book of the Coin).

## Visual symbols

- **The Coin** — two sides, balance and equilibrium; a touchstone with weight.
- **Circle / 360 degrees / Flower of Life** — wholeness, unity, the endless cycle; the complete self.
- **Triangle** — stability, strength, purpose; the three tenets (Strength, Vision, Love) around the center.
- **Son, Brother, Father** — the three stages of a man's life and responsibility. **This is the canonical arc** (see the note in `system/forge-journey.md`).
- **Hourglass** — time and transience; a life is finite, and the sand moves whether he works or not.
- **Plumb bob / Pendulum / Level** — finding center, balance, and true vertical; alignment.
- **Arrow pointing down / Spear pointing down** — grounding and stability; protection and defense.
- **Sun rays / Sun and moon** — life and vitality; the masculine and its complement.
- **Circle of clouds** — unity and the ethereal; the heavens.
- **Four seasons** — the cycle of a life: spring (renewal), summer (fullness), autumn, winter.
- **Axial precession / Solstice azimuth** — the slow wobble and the sun's extremes; direction over long time.
- **Compass rose** — the inner compass, a man's values and true north.
- **Big Dipper & North Star (Polaris)** — the fixed point to navigate by; his Vision.
- **7.2 degree boxes** — the circle divided into **fifty** equal parts of 7.2°, after Eratosthenes, who measured the Earth by reason. (This is the coin's own confirmation of the fifty-segment geometry; forty-five are visible. Book chapter: "The 50 Boxes: 7.2 Degrees.")
- **Azure blue** — the blue of the segment ring (the GAP blue, `#4A73E8`); its meaning is its own chapter in the book.
- **Phi (Φ)** — the golden ratio; proportion and design woven through nature. Its own chapter in the book.
- **Open right hand, palm forward** — honesty, openness, the offer of help; not alone.
- **Standing in the Gap** — the commitment to support, protect, and advocate; the call of Ezekiel 22:30.
- **Fibonacci spiral** — growth and the hero's journey (noted in source, meaning developing).
