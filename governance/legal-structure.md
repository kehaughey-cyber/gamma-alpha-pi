# Legal Structure — the three entities, and what is still open

The institutional architecture GAP is built on, the provenance of the governance
documents (which are canonical and which are not), and the open questions that need a
lawyer before anything is filed. Not legal advice; a map of the current design and its
loose ends.

## The three-entity architecture (canonical)

Per the Constitution (§1.3) and Bylaws (§1.1), GAP operates as three legally distinct
entities so the mission, the brotherhood, and the intellectual property cannot be captured
together:

| Entity | Form | Governs |
|---|---|---|
| **Gamma Alpha Pi Fraternal Brotherhood** | 501(c)(10) domestic fraternal society | the brotherhood, chapters, initiation, curriculum, member-exclusive programs |
| **Gamma Alpha Pi Foundation** | 501(c)(3) public charity | public programs, community benefit, the 3-Day Reset as a public resource, grants, endowment |
| **Gamma Alpha Pi Intellectual Property Company** | for-profit (S-corp or LLC) | owns all IP; licenses it to both nonprofits at arm's-length fair-market rates |

The structural protections live in the governing docs: no private gain, no unilateral
authority, the Non-Acquisition Commitment (Constitution §6.7, unamendable), and IP held
separately so a governance dispute cannot reach it. The AI Governance Codex
(`companion/ai-conduct.md`) is the fourth governing instrument and is consistent with this
structure.

## Provenance — which documents are canonical

Three generations of governance material sit in the quarry. Only one is canon.

- **Canonical (curated here):** `GAP_Institutional_Constitution.docx` + `GAP_Institutional_Bylaws.docx` (both v1.0, Leadership-Council-ratified). Internally consistent, matched to each other and to the AI Governance Codex. → `constitution.md`, `bylaws.md`.
- **Superseded raw ideation (stays in quarry):** `org/CORE ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE.docx` and `org/ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE.docx` are ChatGPT brainstorming transcripts (they literally contain "ChatGPT said:" / "You said:"). Useful history, and the origin of the entity-naming and the Trust idea, but not governance. They propose a **different** structure (see open questions).
- **Not GAP's document (archive; do not use as ours):** `CONSTITUTION AND BYLAWS OF GAMMA ALPHA PI FRATERNITY.docx` (~22k words) is a **collegiate fraternity's** constitution, "As Adopted by the Karnea of 1968, Amended through 2020," with an Arch Chapter, Undergraduate Council, and undergraduate-chapter machinery. It describes a college Greek-letter org, which GAP explicitly is **not** (GAP is non-collegiate and lifelong, men 18+). Treat it as an outside reference only; presenting it as GAP's own governance would be both inaccurate and a hazard.

## Open questions for counsel (do not resolve without a lawyer)

1. **Fraternal entity: 501(c)(10) vs 501(c)(7).** The canonical docs use **(c)(10)** (a fraternal society operating under the lodge system, which can support charitable activity). The older ideation used **(c)(7)** (a social club). These have materially different tax and operational consequences. Confirm which fits the lodge/initiation/charitable model GAP actually intends.
2. **Who owns and controls the for-profit IP Company.** The canonical docs establish the IP Company and its licensing role but do **not** specify its ownership. The older ideation put ownership in an **irrevocable Trust ("GAP Legacy Trust")** with a Protector who can remove trustees, explicitly to lock mission and prevent capture. The canonical structure's protections (Non-Acquisition Commitment, etc.) are contractual/constitutional rather than a trust. Decide whether a trust layer sits above the IP Company, and if so, reconcile its terms with the Constitution.
3. **Entity naming.** Ideation used "GAP Legacy Holdings, LLC" and "GAP Legacy Trust"; the canonical docs use "Intellectual Property Company" generically. Settle the actual registered names.
4. **State of formation and the founding instruments.** Ideation referenced Texas (GAP is based in Dallas). The constitution and bylaws are ratified *templates* with blank signature lines; the actual articles of incorporation, the (c)(10)/(c)(3) determinations, the LLC operating agreement, and any trust instrument are not yet drafted or filed.
