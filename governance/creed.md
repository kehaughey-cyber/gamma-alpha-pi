# The Creed

The founding doctrine of Gamma Alpha Pi. Enshrined in the Constitution (§1.4) as
unamendable except by unanimous Leadership Council vote with a ninety-day deliberation.
This is the single source of the canonical text.

## Canonical form

> I am a servant of God, to my family, and my community. With love, strength, and vision,
> I will act with integrity, grow personally, and uplift, support, and protect my family
> and my community. This is my duty, my privilege, my honor, and my legacy. I am with
> Brothers.

This is the form used everywhere the creed is stated in the third person: the Constitution,
the README, the companion's operating standard.

## Affirmation form (spoken at initiation)

When a man affirms the creed as his own standard, it is spoken in the first person with his
name:

> I, [Name], am a servant of God, to my family, and my community. With love, strength, and
> vision, I will act with integrity, grow personally, and uplift, support, and protect my
> family and my community. This is my duty, my privilege, my honor, and my legacy. I am
> with Brothers.

The two forms are the same creed. The canonical text is the standard; the affirmation form
is how a man takes it on. Source of the affirmation wording: `Fraternity/Creed.docx`.
